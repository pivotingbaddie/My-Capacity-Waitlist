The problem

Most apps assume your day is stable.

But your energy isn't. Some days you're overwhelmed. Some days you're sharp and ready. Most days are somewhere in between.

Traditional task apps don't adjust to that. They expect the same output from you every single day — and they judge you when that doesn't happen.

Survival mode

Your capacity shifts daily. My Capacity reads where you are — and responds accordingly.

The solution

A system that adapts with you.

01
How you feel

Check in with your mood and energy each day. No judgment — just honest input so the app can respond to your actual state.

02
Time you actually have

Tell it how long you've got. Whether it's 20 minutes or a full day, your tasks scale to fit reality — not ambition.

03
What you're building

Set your goals once. The app keeps them in view — weaving them into your daily tasks so you're always moving toward what matters.
